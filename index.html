Career Guidance by Stream

Science Stream

  • Engineering: Requires JEE Main/Advanced. Options: B.Tech, B.Arch.
  • Medical: Requires NEET. Options: MBBS, BDS, B.Pharm.
  • Pure Sciences: B.Sc in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics.

Commerce Stream

  • Chartered Accountancy (CA): Foundation, Intermediate, Final.
  • B.Com / BBA: Management and Finance degrees.
  • CS / CMA: Company Secretary and Cost Management Accounting.

Arts Stream

  • Civil Services: UPSC, OPSC preparation.
  • Law: CLAT for LLB.
  • Journalism & Mass Comm: Bachelor's in Journalism.
